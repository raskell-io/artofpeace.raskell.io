I work with computers and build things. Those habits inform these pages: preparing for mistakes, keeping a failure from spreading, sharing what is useful, and making repair possible.

The peace I seek leaves people free to disagree. It protects independent thought and makes room for the outlier, while asking us to respect one another's boundaries and resist coercion.

The author

I am Raffael Schneider. Raskell is my tech and development blog and web presence; I write this book under my own name.

The form

The book brings together the brevity and strategic attention I admire in Sun Tzu's The Art of War with the warmth and imagery I love in Kahlil Gibran's The Prophet. Its thirteen chapters contain 104 original passages.

This manuscript

This is a first manuscript, developed with AI assistance from my ideas and conversations. It remains open to revision. These passages are principles to examine and practise; their application asks for judgment.

You can read the complete book, visit an individual chapter, or keep a Markdown copy. The complete reading view also has a print layout.

If a passage leaves you with a question, I would be glad to hear it. Write to me.
