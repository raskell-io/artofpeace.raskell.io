Three ways to give the website a more particular identity. Each study includes the full arrival, chapter index, and closing. Reading links open the restored reader in a new tab.

01 · Literary and intimate

Open title spread

A facing-page composition: the thought on the left, the title on the right. A small author signature in the header; a publisher’s colophon at the end.

Open full size ↗

Character A book with a front cover, an inside page, and a colophon. The centre fold gives the landing page its structure.

On a phone The title comes first, followed by the facing-page thought. The chapter index becomes one column.

02 · Spacious and personal

The shoreline

The Nago Bay origin becomes the composition. A horizon crosses the title page, with a dark green footer that feels like arriving at the other shore.

Open full size ↗

Character Closest to the original palette and horizon. A distinctive author mark and a stronger closing give the whole page a rhythm.

On a phone The sun moves partly beyond the edge. The wide horizon remains; the introductory text and chapter index stack.

03 · Direct and editorial

The folio

Large type, ink rules, and a warm rust accent. The header resembles a literary masthead; the footer closes with the author’s signature.

Open full size ↗

Character The words carry the visual identity. A conspicuous chapter index invites browsing directly from the title page.

On a phone The large title stays, with a single-column index and a compact masthead. No extra reader controls.

Three complete compositions. The reader retains its earlier layout.
