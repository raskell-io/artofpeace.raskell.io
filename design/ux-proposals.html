The gesture

Open a related passage in the margin. The connection gives a principle its limits without interrupting the original reading.

Try it

Close the companion, then open it from “Read beside”. Explore the other two pairings. On Phone, the margin becomes an inline section.

The tradeoff

The most distinctive expression of this book. Keep the connections few so the reader has room to interpret the prose.

IV.4

Keep what helps. Question what does not.

The gesture

The reader’s own words sit beside the author’s. Kept passages and questions become a personal route back into the book.

Try it

Keep a passage, write a note, edit it, or remove it and undo. Export creates a text file containing your entries.

The tradeoff

Personal notes need careful handling of storage, export, and changing editions. This is the largest addition to the reader.
